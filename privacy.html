Studio Information

PRIVACY

How information is handled on our website and team workspace.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This notice describes information that may be handled when you visit MarrowVeil Studios online or use the private employee portal. It should be updated when our practices change.

Public website

Browsing the public website does not require an account. The site uses browser local storage to remember whether you dismissed the development notice. GitHub Pages may process technical request data to provide and secure hosting. Some pages load web fonts from Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to connect to Google's servers.

Contact messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use it to respond to your inquiry and manage the conversation. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information by email.

Private employee portal

The portal is for invited team members and uses Supabase authentication and services. Depending on use, information may include account identifiers, display names, tasks, task progress, uploaded file names and files, project labels, notes, submission status, and reviewer feedback. This information supports studio coordination and review.

Access is intended to be restricted through authentication, database permissions, and private file storage. Do not upload personal material unrelated to studio work.

Service providers and retention

Hosting, font delivery, authentication, database, and file-storage providers process information as needed to provide their services. Their privacy terms also apply to data they process. Information may be retained as reasonably needed for inquiries, collaboration, security, and operational recordkeeping. Retention periods depend on the service and studio needs.

Your choices

You may choose not to contact us or use the portal. Team members who need help with account access, correction, or deletion requests should contact the studio administrator. Requests will be reviewed in light of operational needs and applicable requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, email marrowveilstudios@gmail.com.
